22. cat << 'EOF' > ~/systemd_vs_cron.txt
Systemd Timers vs Cron Comparison

1. Service Dependencies:
Systemd timers allow explicit ordering dependencies (After=network.target chronyd.service). Cron attempts execution strictly at the designated system time regardless of network or time sync status.

2. Missed Execution Catch-Up:
Setting 'Persistent=true' in a systemd timer ensures that if the system was powered off at 06:00, the job triggers immediately on the next boot. Cron simply skips missed runs until the next scheduled interval.

3. Logging and Observability:
Timer outputs and failure states log directly to systemd-journald (viewable via 'journalctl -u sentrylog.service'). Cron requires configured mail utilities (MTA) or stdout/stderr redirection to log files.
EOF
